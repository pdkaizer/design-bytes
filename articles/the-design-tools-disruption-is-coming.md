[Muhammad Hassan](https://medium.com/@muhammadhassanmansoori94) wrote an excellent piece about the evolution of design tools and their impact on design. These quotes stood out to me.

> User Experience & Accessibility: Tools may change over time, but understanding human behavior and designing for everyone is forever.

> Problem-Solving Mindset: Good designers are not just good in Figma, Sketch, or Photoshop; they are also good at solving problems faced by their users.

> Designers owe their strength to being creative, adaptable, and having well-versed design knowledge, rather than any specific platform.

> ### In short, great design comes from designers, not tools.
