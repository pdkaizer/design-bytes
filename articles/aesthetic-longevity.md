[Kshitij Kumar](https://medium.com/@infamousdino) has an excellent post on Medium that looks at the fact that:

> Timeless design isn’t just about beauty — it’s rooted in psychology, neuroscience, and cultural perception.

There is a nice straightforward bit about how to do this.

> **Prioritize Simplicity** — Avoid unnecessary details that age quickly.  
>  **Follow Universal Principles** — Use proportions, symmetry, and contrast wisely.  
>  **Design for Humans, Not Trends** — Consider ergonomics and emotional connection. **Allow for Evolution** — Keep the essence while refining elements over time.  
>  **Use High-Quality Materials** — Physical durability contributes to aesthetic longevity.
