This excellent piece by [Krisztina Szerovay](https://medium.com/@krisztina.szerovay) highlights _"the most important balancing act"_  that is an incredibly critical component of excellent digital product design. This quote captures why:

> How to remove or hide everything that’s just noise at a given point of the user flow, and at the same time, keep or display all the content and functionality that’s needed.
